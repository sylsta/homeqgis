about=DomotiQGIS controls a robot vacuum running Valetudo (local, cloud-free) from QGIS, with its map as real GIS layers: live tracking, cleaning by room, group or zone drawn on the map, Go to, rooms and no-go zones edited with the QGIS tools, Valetudo's schedule, camera with recording, Wi-Fi signal surveys and maps, consumables and dock station. It also watches and drives the home servers around it: Raspberry Pi (status, SSH console and log, Wi-Fi maps), Home Assistant (status, console and log), NUC (status, console and log) and Kodi (now playing, play, pause, stop). Settings in one Options page, tokens and passwords encrypted, timestamped backups. Available in English and French.
