about=DomotiQGIS controls a robot vacuum running Valetudo (local, cloud-free) from QGIS, with its map as real GIS layers: live tracking, cleaning by room, group or zone drawn on the map, Go to, rooms and no-go zones edited with the QGIS tools, Valetudo's schedule, cleaning history, camera with recording, Wi-Fi signal surveys and maps, consumables and dock station. A Home section shows the Home Assistant devices by room, with history charts, comparisons, exports and replay on the map. It also watches any number of servers (Raspberry Pi, Home Assistant, Kodi, Linux and Windows computers, web services): status, SSH console and log, remote desktop over VNC or RDP, Kodi playback. Settings in one Options page, tokens and passwords encrypted, timestamped backups, a user manual. Available in English and French.
